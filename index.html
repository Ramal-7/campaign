Why I'm Running

As Media Head, I was at the forefront of every HITS event last year. I've led teams, managed media, directed the departmental magazine, organized events. But I noticed that students leave events with attendance certificates but no idea what they add to a resume or CV. I'll change that by turning every event into something you can actually put on paper.

The Mandate

01

Opportunity Pipeline

Stop hearing about internships after they close.

+

The Problem

Opportunities reach you through random WhatsApp forwards. You find out about internships, competitions, fellowships, and certifications late — or not at all.

Solution

A structured weekly opportunity digest sent directly to HITS members. Partnerships with career services, alumni network, and corporate contacts to source openings first.

What You Get

First access to internships, hackathons, certifications, scholarships, and conferences — organized by field, deadline, and eligibility.

02

Industry Bridge

Build relationships, not just guest lectures.

+

The Problem

Alumni speak once, you take notes, then nothing. No follow-up. No connection. No mentorship.

Solution

Leverage strong UOG alumni connections to build a continuing mentorship network. Monthly sessions with industry experts. Office hours, project feedback, career guidance — working relationships, not lectures.

What You Get

Direct access to professionals in your target industry. Real feedback on your projects. Connections that extend beyond graduation.

What You Get

Certificates that actually mean something. LinkedIn-ready credentials. Portfolio pieces. Demonstrated competence, not just attendance records.

Legacy System

What You Get

A society that improves continuously. Systems that work better each year. The infrastructure I build becomes the foundation for whoever comes next.
